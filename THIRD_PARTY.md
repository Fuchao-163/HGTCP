# Third-party components and datasets

The training code depends on PyTorch, PyTorch Geometric, OGB, NetworkX,
PyMETIS, NumPy, SciPy, scikit-learn, pandas, einops, and tqdm. Their licenses
remain with their respective authors.

Dataset loaders download public benchmarks from their original providers.
No dataset is redistributed here. Users are responsible for accepting the
terms of each dataset provider.

Parts of the original large-graph training pipeline were developed from the
public SGFormer/GOAT-style experimental infrastructure. Before public release,
the authors should verify the exact upstream commits and copy every notice
required by those repositories' licenses.
